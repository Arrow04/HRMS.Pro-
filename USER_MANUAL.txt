HRMS.Pro! — User Manual
========================

1. INTRODUCTION
---------------
Welcome to HRMS.Pro!, your all-in-one Human Resource Management System.
This manual will help you navigate and use the system effectively, whether
you are an employee, manager, HR administrator, or superadmin.

The system consists of:
  - Web Portal (hrms_react_web)   : Desktop/browser access
  - Mobile App (hrms_mobile)      : Android/iOS self-service
  - Control Hub (hrms_tenant_hub) : Superadmin tenant management


2. GETTING STARTED
------------------
2.1 Logging In

  Web Portal:
    1. Open your browser and go to the web portal URL provided by your HR.
    2. Enter your email and password.
    3. Click "Sign In".
    4. If you forget your password, use the "Forgot Password" link.

  Mobile App:
    1. Install the HRMS.Pro! app from the Play Store / App Store.
    2. Open the app and enter your company server URL if prompted.
    3. Sign in with your email and password.
    4. Enable biometric login for faster access (Face ID / fingerprint).

  First-time login:
    - Your account is created by your HR administrator.
    - You will receive an invitation email with your temporary password.
    - You will be asked to change your password on first login.


3. EMPLOYEE SELF-SERVICE
------------------------
3.1 Dashboard

  After logging in, you will see your personalized dashboard showing:
    - Attendance summary for today
    - Leave balance overview
    - Pending approvals (if you are a manager)
    - Upcoming holidays
    - Recent announcements

3.2 Attendance

  Mark Attendance (Web):
    1. Go to Attendance > Mark Attendance.
    2. Select check-in or check-out.
    3. Upload a selfie for verification if required.
    4. Submit.

  Mark Attendance (Mobile):
    1. Open the app and go to the Attendance tab.
    2. Tap "Check In" or "Check Out".
    3. Take a selfie if prompted.
    4. Your attendance is recorded with GPS and timestamp.

  View Attendance History:
    1. Go to Attendance > History.
    2. Use filters to view by month, week, or date range.
    3. Export attendance report as PDF or Excel.

  Attendance Correction:
    - If there is a missed punch, submit a correction request.
    - Your manager/HR will review and approve it.

3.3 Leave Management

  Apply for Leave:
    1. Go to Leaves > Apply for Leave.
    2. Select leave type (Annual, Sick, Personal, etc.).
    3. Choose start and end dates.
    4. Enter a reason.
    5. Submit for approval.

  Track Leave Status:
    - View all leave applications and their current status.
    - Statuses: Pending, Approved, Rejected, Cancelled.

  Leave Balance:
    - View your remaining leave balance for each type.
    - Balances are updated automatically upon approval.

3.4 Expenses

  Submit Expense:
    1. Go to Expenses > New Expense.
    2. Enter expense details (category, amount, date, description).
    3. Upload supporting documents/receipts.
    4. Submit for approval.

  Expense Status:
    - Track submitted expenses and reimbursement status.
    - Finance will process approved expenses in the next payroll cycle.

3.5 Payslips

  View Payslip:
    1. Go to Payroll > Payslips.
    2. Select the month and year.
    3. View detailed salary breakdown.
    4. Download as PDF.

3.6 Profile

  Update Profile:
    1. Go to My Profile.
    2. Update personal information, contact details, emergency contacts.
    3. Upload profile photo and documents.
    4. Save changes.

  Documents:
    - View and download your stored documents (offer letter, ID proof, etc.).
    - Upload new documents if required.


4. MANAGER / HR FEATURES
------------------------
4.1 Employee Management

  View Employees:
    1. Go to Employees > Employee List.
    2. Search, filter, and sort employees.
    3. View detailed employee profiles.

  Add Employee:
    1. Click "Add Employee".
    2. Fill in personal details, job information, and assign department/designation.
    3. Set employment type and join date.
    4. System generates employee code automatically.

  Edit Employee:
    1. Click on an employee record.
    2. Update details as needed.
    3. Save changes.

4.2 Leave Approval

  1. Go to Approvals > Leave Requests.
  2. View pending leave requests from your team.
  3. Review leave balance and attendance history.
  4. Approve or reject with optional comments.

4.3 Attendance Management

  Attendance Policies:
    - Configure company attendance rules, shifts, and grace periods.

  Attendance Correction:
    - Review and approve/reject correction requests.
    - Bulk update attendance if needed.

  Reports:
    - Generate attendance reports by department, date range, or employee.
    - Export to Excel or PDF.

4.4 Payroll Processing

  Payroll Configuration:
    1. Go to Payroll > Configuration.
    2. Set up salary components (basic, HRA, allowances, deductions).
    3. Configure statutory compliance (PF, ESI, NPS, LWF, Professional Tax).
    4. Define tax regimes and slabs.

  Run Payroll:
    1. Go to Payroll > Run Payroll.
    2. Select month and year.
    3. Review attendance and leave data.
    4. Process payroll for selected employees.
    5. Review and publish payslips.

  Payroll Reports:
    - Generate salary registers, PF/ESI reports, tax reports.
    - Export for accounting and compliance.

4.5 Recruitment

  Job Postings:
    1. Go to Recruitment > Job Openings.
    2. Create new job posting with description, requirements, and deadline.
    3. Publish on job portals if integrated.

  Candidate Management:
    1. View applications and resumes.
    2. Schedule interviews.
    3. Record interview feedback.
    4. Move candidates through pipeline stages.

  Offer Letter:
    - Generate offer letters for selected candidates.
    - Track acceptance and onboarding.

4.6 Performance Management

  Performance Reviews:
    1. Go to Performance > Reviews.
    2. Create review cycles.
    3. Assign reviewers and set goals.
    4. Collect self-assessments and manager ratings.
    5. Generate performance reports.

4.7 Asset Management

  Assign Assets:
    1. Go to Assets > Asset List.
    2. Add new assets (laptop, mobile, etc.).
    3. Assign to employees with issue and return dates.
    4. Track asset condition and maintenance.

4.8 Reports

  Standard Reports:
    - Employee reports (joiners, leavers, headcount)
    - Attendance reports
    - Leave reports
    - Payroll reports
    - Expense reports
    - Recruitment reports

  Custom Reports:
    - Use the Reports module to create custom reports.
    - Schedule automated report generation and email delivery.

  Export:
    - All reports can be exported to PDF, Excel, or CSV.


5. SUPERADMIN / CONTROL HUB
----------------------------
5.1 Tenant Management

  View Tenants:
    - See all registered organizations/tenants.
    - Monitor subscription status and usage.

  Create Tenant:
    1. Click "Register Tenant".
    2. Enter organization details, plan, and admin user.
    3. System provisions tenant database/schema.

  Manage Tenant:
    - Update tenant settings, plan, and status.
    - View tenant-specific metrics and health.

5.2 Billing & Subscriptions

  Plans:
    - Define pricing plans (monthly/yearly per user).
    - Set feature limits per plan.

  Invoices:
    - Generate and send invoices to tenants.
    - Track payment status.

  Feature Flags:
    - Enable/disable features per tenant or globally.

5.3 System Health

  Monitoring:
    - View API response times, error rates, and uptime.
    - Monitor database and Redis health.

  Audit Logs:
    - Review system-wide audit trail for compliance.

5.4 Superadmin Dashboard

  - High-level metrics across all tenants.
  - Revenue and growth tracking.
  - Recent activities and alerts.

7. COMMON WORKFLOWS
-------------------
7.1 New Employee Onboarding
  1. HR adds employee record in the system.
  2. System sends invitation email.
  3. Employee sets password and logs in.
  4. Employee completes profile and uploads documents.
  5. HR assigns assets and training.

7.2 Leave Request Flow
  1. Employee submits leave request.
  2. Manager receives notification and reviews.
  3. Manager approves or rejects.
  4. Employee is notified.
  5. Leave balance is updated automatically.
  6. Payroll system considers approved leave.

7.3 Payroll Flow
  1. HR configures payroll policies and components.
  2. Attendance and leave data is locked for the month.
  3. HR runs payroll for selected employees.
  4. System calculates gross, deductions, and net pay.
  5. HR reviews and publishes payslips.
  6. Employees receive notifications and can download payslips.

7.4 Recruitment Flow
  1. Manager creates job opening.
  2. HR publishes and shares job posting.
  3. Candidates apply with resume.
  4. HR screens and schedules interviews.
  5. Interviewers provide feedback.
  6. HR selects candidate and sends offer.
  7. Candidate accepts and joins.


8. TROUBLESHOOTING
------------------
Cannot log in:
  - Verify your email and password.
  - Use "Forgot Password" to reset.
  - Contact HR if your account is locked.

Attendance not marked:
  - Check GPS and internet connectivity.
  - Ensure company policy allows mobile attendance.
  - Submit correction request if missed.

Leave not approved:
  - Check with your manager for pending approval.
  - Ensure you have sufficient leave balance.
  - Verify dates do not conflict with holidays/shifts.

Mobile app crashes:
  - Update to the latest version.
  - Clear app cache and restart.
  - Reinstall if issue persists.
